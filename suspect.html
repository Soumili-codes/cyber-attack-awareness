How to Identify a Suspicious Cyber Attack

Watch Out for These Signs

  • Unexpected emails or messages asking for personal information.
  • Links from unknown or suspicious sources.
  • Messages creating urgency or threats.
  • Requests for passwords, OTPs, or financial information.
  • Unexpected downloads or attachments.

What Should You Do?

  • Do not click suspicious links or attachments.
  • Do not share passwords or OTPs.
  • Verify the sender before responding.
  • Report suspicious activity.
